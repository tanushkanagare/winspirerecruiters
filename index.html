Our Process

How our recruitment process works

Two clear journeys — one for companies hiring talent, one for candidates seeking opportunity.

1

Share Your Requirement

The company shares the job description, hiring requirement, experience, location, salary range, and other expectations.

2

Requirement Understanding

Winspire Recruiters understands the role, company culture, technical requirements, and ideal candidate profile.

3

Candidate Sourcing

Relevant candidates are identified through our recruitment network and sourcing channels.

4

Screening & Shortlisting

Candidates are screened based on skills, experience, communication, and job suitability.

5

Candidate Profiles Shared

Relevant shortlisted candidates are presented to the client for further evaluation.

6

Interview Coordination

Winspire coordinates communication and interview scheduling between the candidate and the client.

7

Background Verification

Identity, employment, education, reference, and document verification are conducted to ensure candidate authenticity.

8

Selection & Offer

The client selects the suitable candidate and proceeds with the offer and joining process.

9

Joining & Follow-Up

Winspire supports the process until joining and maintains follow-up after placement.
